# Keyword Pro v1.0.0-rc.1

Keyword Pro v1.0.0-rc.1 is the first public release candidate of the
local-first keyword research console.

## Highlights

- Run a guided report across up to 19 keyword sources, with demand, CPC,
  difficulty, intent, trends, SERP competitors, and related searches.
- Choose from 94 markets and 46 languages. Invalid country-language pairs are
  blocked before they can reach a paid provider request.
- Explore 332 keyword endpoints in Advanced mode.
- Save completed reports locally, then reopen them without another provider
  call.
- Export keyword results as PDF, CSV, and JSON.
- Bring your own DataForSEO credentials through local environment settings or
  the Connections screen.
- Review eight real application screenshots generated from deterministic
  synthetic data with no account or provider-response data.

## Privacy and supported use

Credentials stay on the local server, saved values are encrypted at rest, and
the application binds to loopback by default. This release is designed for one
local user. It is not a hardened multi-user internet service.

Live research spends money from the connected DataForSEO account. Keyword Pro
shows an estimate before a run, and actual cost and coverage vary by query,
market, language, account access, and provider response.
